Chapter 37 — In a minor game, one character transforms into three; in theater-title antiphony, two words expand into four.

To return to our story: Qinyan had returned to his lodgings in a fury. He wept for half the day before lying down on his bed fully clothed, pulling the quilt over himself. He regretted a thousand times over having gone to see Pincai, realizing too late that Pincai was in league with the rest of them and had deliberately set out to humiliate him. So humiliated was he that he wished he could die. He went on seething and weeping through half the night. Morning came and he was still asleep, so his young manservant had to come in and shake him awake. "Mr Xu at the Garden of Contentment has sent for you," the boy said. "He says to come quickly. Young Master Mei is already there." Qinyan got up, and the boy folded away the bedding while Qinyan washed his face and drank a bowl of tea. After spending the previous day in a rage and half the night in tears, following two days of exhaustion, he now felt dizzy, with blurred vision. His mouth was dry and he felt utterly wretched. He struggled to rally himself, changed his clothes, and glanced at himself in the mirror, noticing that he looked a little thinner than usual. He sat for a while longer feeling listless. At the beginning of the noon watch, he made himself get into a carriage and set off for the Garden of Contentment.

It was the first day of the second month. In the garden not all the plum blossoms had opened, though the camellia and magnolia were in bloom.

For today's gathering, Liu Wenze, Yan Zhongqing, and Tian Chunhang did not attend. Chunhang was attending a New Year gathering of his examination year-mates. The examination cohort had invited Wenze and Wang Xun as sons of their examiners. Chunhang had also invited Zhongqing, but Zhongqing had recently caught a chill, so he declined both invitations. Wang Xun also declined his invitation, having arranged early in the morning to accompany Ziyu to the Garden of Contentment. Cixian and Ziyun welcomed them into the Plum Knoll and they took their seats. This retreat was laid out in the shape of a plum blossom, comprising five sections of five rooms each. Winding corridors and curved balustrades connected them, surrounded by a sea of green calyxes and crimson fragrance. From outside, one could scarcely make out the buildings: all was fragrant snow. Whenever Ziyu visited the garden, he always took time to admire several such spots. Ziyun said, "We are rather short of company today, as there are many theatricals and banquets this month. Yunong still has more than twenty days before he must return to the Hua household, and these plum blossoms should last another ten days. I mean to keep open house for ten days. Whoever is free can come, whether we are many or few. Even if I happen to be occupied outside the garden, Jingyi will always be here to act as host. What do Yong'an and Yuxiang think of this?" Wang Xun replied, "That sounds ideal. I shall certainly come whenever I have the time." Ziyu said, "If I may say so, our host need not trouble himself every single day. Whoever feels the inclination might simply bring his wine and seek your company and instruction, or perhaps we might find some other diversion." Cixian added, "There are plenty of ways to pass the time. It is just that none of us has a consuming taste for any of them, so our pleasures are rather quieter than most people’s. For instance, some people gather to play cards or roll dice, or even gamble at bao and fantan. Others might beat gongs and drums, watch conjuring tricks, or listen to blind storytellers; these are all common amusements. Failing that, they might hire a theatrical troupe to perform, filling the stage with spears and swords and tumbling acrobats, then put on theatrical headgear and makeup themselves, go onstage to sing a scene, and come off looking thoroughly pleased with themselves. That too is a way to pass the time. And then there are the brothels of the pleasure quarter, where one might embrace powdered faces and oiled hair, flirting and jesting in a great tumult. Not only am I incapable of such things, but I expect none of you gentlemen care for them either." Ziyun and the others murmured their agreement, noting that by this measure they could hardly be called lovers of excitement. Yet they agreed that nothing in the world brought greater joy than drinking wine and admiring flowers. Wang Xun turned to Ziyun and said, "I have a question for you to judge." Ziyun invited him to speak. Wang Xun asked, "Between the flower among men and the flower among flowers, which is the more beautiful?" Ziyun smiled and replied, "Each has its own beauty." Wang Xun pressed, "If one cannot have both, does one choose the person or the flower?" Ziyun laughed, "What nonsense! People are worth more than flowers, of course. Why even ask?" Cixian said, "He must have something in mind. This is no idle question."

Ziyun smiled faintly. Wang Xun laughed, "I see your garden is filled with blossoms, yet in all the time we have been talking not a single flower among men has appeared. Does this not show you prefer flowers to people?" Ziyun replied with a chuckle, "So that is what you mean! We made perfectly good arrangements the other day, so why have they not arrived yet?" A short while later, Baozhu and Guibao arrived and exchanged greetings. Ziyun asked, "Only the two of you, even at this hour?" Baozhu answered, "I fear some may be unable to attend today. Has Yunong not arrived either?" Guibao explained, "Today Lianjin has five private engagements and Lianzhu has four. They are probably all performing Kunqu, and there are not enough performers to cover all the engagements. We have each already performed at one gathering." Wang Xun asked why there were so many engagements today, to which Guibao replied that they would be busy for another half month before things quieted down. Baozhu added, "I saw Xiangfan and Qianzhou there; why did Jiantan not come?" Wang Xun explained that he was unwell. Guibao turned to Ziyu and said, "This is the first time we have met this year." Ziyu agreed, noting that although he had been out several times, he had never run into him. Baozhu said, "Xiangwan and Jingfang are having a hard time of it today, as they are scheduled to perform everywhere and simply cannot get away." Ziyun said, "I thought I could count on six or seven people, but who could have known they would all be unavailable?" As midday approached, Guibao looked out and announced, "Yunong is here!" Everyone turned to watch him enter. Ziyu saw that he had grown considerably taller since last year. Dressed in elegantly plain clothes as he walked through the plum grove, he and the blossoms seemed all of a piece, bright and dazzling. The company welcomed him with smiles. Qinyan first greeted Cixian, Ziyun, and Wang Xun, then turned to Ziyu to exchange polite greetings. Ziyun laughed and said, "He has grown taller now and his learning has advanced. Look at him exchanging pleasantries with Yuxiang; he would hardly have been able to do that last year." Hearing this, Qinyan felt somewhat embarrassed and replied, "I have not seen him for half a year." Ziyun teased, "And when did we ever see each other often?" Qinyan smiled, "Did we not see each other when you and Jingyi came to pay New Year calls?" Cixian laughed, "Ah, so once you have seen someone, there is nothing more to say to him!" This made Qinyan laugh. Wang Xun added, "I am the one who sees Yunong the least." Qinyan nodded in agreement, then sat down alongside Baozhu and Guibao. Baozhu urged him to take the upper seat, which he did.

Ziyun gave orders for the table to be laid, without a formal offering of wine. He said to Wang Xun, "You are older than Yuxiang, but today we are drinking in Yunong's honour. Let him take the seat of honour, with Yuxiang beside him." Qinyan protested, "That cannot be. I shall not sit there." Ziyu suggested that Yong'an should have the top seat. Ziyun replied, "On other occasions, yes, but today we are reversing the order." He took Qinyan by the arm and settled him firmly in the seat of honour, placing Ziyu beside him. Guibao took the second seat, with Wang Xun alongside. There was to be no more yielding places: anyone who tried would have to drink ten cups. Ziyun then called on Baozhu to sit in the upper position. Just as Baozhu was about to decline, Huifang arrived. Ziyun welcomed him warmly, "Splendid, do sit down, Cixian will be beside you." Huifang was reluctant to sit above Cixian, but Cixian reassured him, "Today's arrangement puts all of you above us. Have you not seen that two have already been settled?" Huifang had to comply, and Baozhu below likewise had no choice but to take the seat above Ziyun. Once everyone was seated, Wang Xun laughed and said, "With this seating order at a restaurant, the bill would have to be paid the other way round." Everyone smiled, toasted one another a few rounds, and leisurely enjoyed a few dishes.

Baozhu noticed that Qinyan's eyes looked swollen from weeping and guessed it must be on account of his teacher. Ziyun had also noticed this and sighed quietly. "Yunong is truly a tender-hearted soul," he said. "Changqing did not treat him particularly well, and yet he has wept like this for him; that is rare indeed." Everyone present sighed in sympathy. When Qinyan heard this, it stirred up yesterday's resentment, casting an expression of anger across his face. Then he saw Ziyu beside him: it had all come of his wish to see Ziyu. A surge of anguish welled up within him, and tears spilled down his cheeks. Everyone at the table hastened to offer words of comfort, not realizing that Qinyan's grief had quite a different source and had nothing whatsoever to do with Changqing. Since they did not know the truth and he could hardly explain it to them, he kept it all locked inside himself. The more he brooded, the angrier he grew, until he could bear it no longer. Pressing his handkerchief to his face, he thought to himself: "That wretch Wei Pincai is always inventing rumours. He is sure to say I kept them company over wine at his lodgings, that Xi Eleven rewarded me with a bracelet, and so on. It would be better to speak up myself and let people know the truth. Besides, surely no one here would laugh at me." He waited another moment, then asked Ziyu, "When did you last see Pincai?" Ziyu replied, "Not since sometime in the tenth month of last year. He is living now at Hongji Temple outside the city wall and never comes to my house."

Qinyan said, "I saw him yesterday and he told me he had met you three times this year." Ziyu replied, "That is quite untrue. The most absurd instance was at dawn on New Year's Day when he came banging on the gate. The doorman had only just gotten out of bed and dressed. Pincai said a word or two, left a calling card, and to this day we have not seen each other. Where did you see him?" Qinyan cursed, "That Wei Pincai has never been anything but a scoundrel." Huifang agreed, "He was a scoundrel long before that. You needn't tell us." Ziyu pressed Qinyan to continue. With tears in his eyes, Qinyan confessed, "The fault was mine. I went to his lodgings and asked him to come with me to see you." At these words a sharp pang went through Ziyu, and his eyes began to redden. Everyone listened intently as Qinyan continued. Wang Xun asked, "And how did he treat you?" Qinyan explained, "Pincai used to be decent enough, but now a set of bad characters are leading him astray." Ziyun asked who they were. Qinyan said, "One is Xi Eleven, another is Pan Qiguan, and a monk—Pincai's landlord."

Huifang frowned and asked, "And what happened to you?" Qinyan's anger had by now reached such a pitch that he went ahead and recounted the whole story in detail: how Xi Eleven had made a point of leaving early, how Pincai had then sent Pan the Third away, how Xi Eleven had unexpectedly sent over some dishes, and how, one after another, Xi Eleven, Pan the Third, and the monk had all barged in. He detailed every manner of insult and mockery they had subjected him to at the table, and how he had smashed the bracelet Xi had forced onto his wrist. Listening to this, Ziyu was furious. "This is all Pincai's doing," he said. "He must have arranged the whole thing on purpose, deliberately setting those men on to ill-use you." Qinyan agreed, "Of course he was in it with them. Fortunately, I no longer perform on stage, so they dared not go too far. Otherwise it would have been far worse, and I doubt you would be seeing me here today." Ziyun said, "What bad luck to run into all three of those fiends at once! You must have had a dreadful time." Cixian and Wang Xun both laughed. Guibao added, "That Xi Eleven, I have never had dealings with him myself, but Peixian and Yuyan suffered badly at his hands." Qinyan noted, "For me it has been twice now." Wang Xun asked Guibao what he would do if he ran into Xi Eleven. Guibao replied, "If I ran into him, I would have him take a look at the barrel too and let a carter have his turn with him." Everyone burst out laughing. Huifang wondered aloud, "What scheme could we devise to deal with him?" Cixian laughed and suggested, "If you want to deal with him, you'll have to risk your own skin as bait. I doubt you'd agree to that." Huifang spat in contempt, prompting Cixian to laugh again. Ziyun asked what he found so amusing. Cixian explained, "It’s that thing of Xi Eleven’s that causes all the trouble. Why not have it cut off? Then he’d behave." Wang Xun laughed, "That would be no easy matter; it could only be done if Meixiang were willing to risk his own skin as bait." Huifang retorted, "Why do you not do it yourself?" Wang Xun replied, "I have no quarrel with him. Why should I cut him? Never mind cutting Xi Eleven, cut Pan the Third first and you would spare yourself a great deal of terror." Huifang spat several times in disgust, then suddenly poured a cup of wine for Cixian as a penalty, saying, "It is entirely your fault; who told you to bring up those people?" Cixian accepted the cup without demur, smiled, and drank it down.

Just then, Ziyu and Qinyan caught each other's gaze, and each drank half a cup. Ziyun smiled and asked Ziyu, "How many times have you and Yunong sat at the same table now?" Ziyu replied, "This is the second time, and it has been a year since the first." Ziyun lamented, "Only twice? What a pity, what a pity! You meet so rarely and part so often." Qinyan smiled and corrected him, "This is in fact the third time." Cixian said, "Yuxiang is being a little greedy, counting fewer than there have been. Last year you two slipped away to stroll along the canal without telling anyone; does that not count?" Ziyu admitted, "It slipped my mind." Ziyun then proposed, "I should like to invite you and Yunong to enjoy ten days of happiness together. I trust you will not find it tiresome?" Ziyu responded, "A fine garden and good friends—what greater pleasure than to meet here often? How could I find that tiresome? I only fear I may not have the good fortune to enjoy such delightful company, and that ill luck might bring disaster."

Ziyun laughed heartily. Cixian quipped, "Even ten days together are too much good fortune for him! What if he had Hua Xingbei's luck? Now that would be good fortune as vast as the Eastern Sea!" Everyone laughed at this remark. By now, Qinyan and Ziyu were both filled with contentment.

Wang Guibao turned to Ziyun with a smile and proposed, "I have a game: turn one character into three. I'll show you how. Anyone who can't give another must drink a cup." Ziyun invited him to proceed. Guibao explained, "Take the character for 'great' (大) and add a dot to make it 'extreme' (太). Move the dot to the top and it becomes 'dog' (犬). Now, everyone must offer a sequence just like this." Ziyun laughed, "This is a dog's game; who has the patience for it?" Guibao grinned at Huifang and prompted, "You give one." Huifang thought for a moment and offered, "Take the character for 'king' (王) and add a dot to make it 'jade' (玉). Move the dot to the top and it becomes 'host' (主). Is that not better than your dog?" Guibao nodded in agreement, "Very good." Then he suddenly laughed again, "Now look what you've done! Duxiang just insulted me, and you've insulted him in turn." Huifang was puzzled, "When did I scold him?" The others were equally confused, so Guibao explained, "He is the host, and you joined your word 'host' to my word 'dog'. Isn't that an insult to him?" Huifang smiled, while Ziyun chided Guibao, "You little fox-spirit! You've been full of mischief lately, with that slick tongue of yours." Baozhu chimed in, "I have one: take the character for 'wood' (木), add a stroke to make it 'root' (本), and move it to the top to make it 'not yet' (未)." Ziyun laughed, "I have a variation on that: take 'not yet' (未), remove a stroke to make 'wood' (木), and move it to the bottom to make 'root' (本)." Everyone burst into laughter.

Qinyan offered, "I have the character for 'water' (水), add a dot to make it 'ice' (氷), and move it to the top to make it 'eternal' (永)." Cixian said, "That 永 is not quite right; it will only pass as thin ice. Still, there aren't many examples that come readily to mind." Wang Xun said, "I fear there are only a few, and they have all been taken." Guibao proposed another, "I have the character for 'ten' (十), add a stroke to make it 'scholar' (士), and move it to the top to make it 'shield' (干)." Everyone approved. Huifang added, "I have the character for 'dark' (杳), add a stroke to make it 'investigate' (查), and move it to the top to make it 'fragrant' (香)." The company praised this one even more highly. Baozhu suggested, "I have the character 丁, add a stroke to make it 'in' (于), and move it to the top to make it 'step' (亍)." Ziyun said, "That character is rather obscure." Ziyu countered, "It is acceptable enough." Baozhu defended it, "彳 and 亍 aren't so very obscure." Qinyan offered another, "I have the character for 'divine' (卜), add a stroke to make it 'above' (上), and move it to the top to make it 'below' (下)." Cixian said, "That is very good." Guibao said, "I have the character for 'white' (白), add a stroke to make it 'self' (自), and move it to the top to make it 'hundred' (百)." Huifang said, "A little short." Wang Xun then offered, "I have the character for 'speak' (曰), add a stroke to make it 'field' (田), and move it to the top..." He paused mid-sentence. Guibao prompted, "Move it to the top and it becomes what character?" Wang Xun burst out laughing, and Ziyu explained, "If you push it straight through to the top, it becomes 'reason' (由)." Ziyun added, "I would pull it down to make it 'armour' (甲)." Cixian laughed, "One of you wants it up, the other wants it down. You'll end up quarrelling! I say let it go up at one end and down at the other, making the character 'explain' (申). How is that?" Everyone burst into laughter.

They ate a few pastries and drank several more cups of wine. Wang Xun asked Huifang, "Have you seen Xiangfan and Qianzhou?" Huifang replied, "It was because they were there that I was held up for so long. My performance was moved earlier so I could come. I saw a remarkable old gentleman of letters there today, said to be Qianzhou's teacher. He had a refined, old-fashioned air and appeared to be over sixty." Ziyun asked his surname. Huifang answered, "It is an unusual name. Let me think, something like Qu, I believe. He was wearing sixth-rank official dress, and his conversation was so brilliant and wide-ranging that everyone present held him in admiration." Ziyun thought for a moment and corrected him, "It will be Qu written 屈, not Qu written 瞿." Huifang agreed, "Yes, Qu, I had it wrong." Cixian asked, "Could it be Qu Daosheng?" Ziyun confirmed, "It must be him. I heard he’d arrived." Ziyu asked, "Would his given name be Benli?" Ziyun said, "Exactly! Do you know him?" Ziyu explained, "I do not know him personally, but I have read several of his letters to my father, discussing obscure points of history. In them, he advanced wholly original views that illuminated the ages. My father had them mounted in an album, which I often look through." Cixian said, "Master Daosheng is sixty years old this year. He and my late elder brother were both recommended under the xiaolian fangzheng category, for filial devotion, integrity and upright conduct. He served as a county magistrate in Jiangxi; what brings him to the capital?" Ziyun replied, "He was promoted to Assistant Prefect last year and has presumably come to be presented at court. One of these days I shall invite him here and we can all meet him. He may be a man of principle, but he is also very fond of flowers and wine." Ziyu said, "He is a friend of my father's. I fear I might not be a suitable companion for him." Ziyun reassured him, "What harm could there be?" Cixian added, "Daosheng may be set in his old-fashioned ways, but his writing is very playful. Besides his major works, he has produced various miscellaneous pieces, including one collection called *Forgetting Death* and another called *The Awakening from Sleep Collection*, all of it written in a spirit of amusement." Qinyan said, "Those two titles alone are remarkable." Wang Xun asked, "What is in them?"

Cixian said, "I once flipped through them briefly on someone's desk, but did not read them closely. I recall that *The Awakening from Sleep Collection* (醒睡集) contained pieces composed by collaging lines from ci poems to make new ci, and from qu lyrics to make new qu, as well as couplets assembled from lines in the classics, shi poetry and ci lyrics. It was quite amusing. I believe towards the end there was a section of couplets matching opera titles, most of them two-character titles, but I regret I did not examine it carefully." Ziyun asked, "How would you compare Daosheng's poetry and prose with Hou Shiweng's?" Cixian said, "In my estimation, Daoweng is the superior of the two. Shiweng has great talent, but his writing lacks distinction and is rather mixed and impure. Daoweng, too, is a man of considerable talent, and his writing has a pure, sound style; his works deserve to be handed down. Even Shiweng himself greatly admires him." Wang Xun asked, "Do you mean our Nanjing man, Hou Shiweng? He considers himself the foremost genius under heaven, but from what I have seen, I am not sure that is warranted." Ziyun said, "The talent is great and the learning is broad. To achieve what he has achieved is no small thing." Then he proposed a new game, "Opera titles would make good couplets. For instance, *Watching a Painting* (观画) could be paired with *Stealing the Poem* (偷诗), and *Stealing the Poem* (偷诗) could also be paired with *Picking Up a Painting* (拾画). It is a rather amusing game. Let's divide into four pairs. I'll match a title you give me, then you match one of mine. One character poorly matched costs a cup; two cost two cups; a partly imperfect match, half a cup. A particularly clever or perfect match deserves a toast."

Cixian agreed, "Excellent. Let Yuxiang and Yunong start." Ziyu suggested, "It would be better if you and Meixiang go first, then Duxiang and Yaoqing, then Yong'an and Ruixiang, and we shall go last." Ziyun agreed to this arrangement, "You take the vanguard, and he brings up the rear; that leaves us in the middle, where it is easier to make a good showing." Cixian said, "Hard to begin, hard to begin! I can't think of a good one just now. The day before yesterday I saw Shouxiang perform *Inscribing the Song* (题曲) and he sang it beautifully, so I shall take that as my opening." Huifang immediately responded, "*Inscribing the Song* (题曲) could certainly be paired with *Stealing the Poem* (偷诗)." Baozhu interjected, "You’ve just picked an answer someone else already used. The pairing is muddled, so the penalty is one cup." Huifang protested, "Then it doesn't count—why should I be penalized?" Ziyun insisted, "You must drink. Otherwise we'll do nothing but match titles and never touch the wine." Thus, Huifang was made to drink a cup as forfeit. After a moment of thought, Huifang offered, "Will *Teaching Song* (教歌) do as a pairing?" Cixian approved, "Good." Everyone agreed it was a fitting match. Huifang said, "If it's good, it deserves a toast." Ziyun said, "Indeed it does." A toast was drunk by all at the table. Huifang then put forward *Burying the Jade* (埋玉), and Cixian matched it with *Finding Gold* (拾金). Wang Xun praised it, "That is perfectly matched, let us toast again." And so each person toasted again.

It was now Ziyun's turn to put forward a title, and he offered the first half of a couplet: *Treading on the Moon* (踏月). Baozhu thought for a moment and paired it with *Sweeping Flowers* (扫花). Guibao said, "Excellent." Ziyun said, "The pairing is good in terms of meaning, but when two-character opera titles are matched, the level and oblique tones should contrast. 扫 (sweep), like 踏 (tread), has an oblique tone, so there is a slight imperfection here." Cixian said, "You're right. There are plenty of opera titles, but very few that pair well. The second characters must contrast in level and oblique tone, but we can allow some leeway with the first. This is unlike a general couplet matching historical references, where one may range freely in search of material; here the choices are finite. It is better to have a pairing where the characters match well but the tones are slightly off than one where the tones are perfect but the characters are weak. As for the second character, no error can be excused." Ziyun reflected on this, agreed that the pickings were indeed slim, and accepted the answer. Baozhu then put forward *The Monastery Gate* (山门), and after some deliberation Ziyun matched it with *The Stone Grotto* (石洞). This was deemed acceptably well-matched, earning a toast. Next came the turn of Wang Xun and Guibao. Wang Xun offered *Singing the Ballad* (弹词), and Guibao matched it with *Composing the Score* (制谱). Cixian said, "The opening title ought to be fresh. Too straightforward, and there's no surprise." Guibao turned to Wang Xun and said, "Let me give you a fresh one: *Stealing the Chicken* (偷鸡)." Wang Xun replied, "I shall match it with *Subduing the Tiger* (伏虎)." Everyone praised this as a fine match and called for a toast. Cixian said, "It is indeed a good pairing, but *Stealing the Chicken* (偷鸡) is small and petty, while *Subduing the Tiger* (伏虎) is bold and grand. What do you all think?" Wang Xun acknowledged the point, "You're quite right. I shall change mine to *Visiting the Rat* (访鼠)." Cixian said, "Now that deserves a toast." Everyone drank. When it came to Ziyu's turn, he offered *Looking at the Stocking* (看袜), and Qinyan paired it with *Borrowing the Boot* (借靴). The company exclaimed, "Beautifully paired! That deserves two toasts." Huifang interjected, "One cup is enough. That was a quick answer, too. If we keep drinking two cups at a time, we'll all be too drunk to make good matches." Cixian agreed, "Quite right. Henceforth, we shall have a full toast for the very best pairings, half a cup for good ones, and nothing for ordinary ones." And so each person drank a cup.

Qinyan then put forward *The Drunken Consort* (醉妃). Wang Xun's earlier title, *Subduing the Tiger* (伏虎), gave Ziyu an idea, and he answered Qinyan with *Sobering the Courtesan* (醒妓). Everyone exclaimed, "That pairing is delightful! A full cup all round!" Qinyan added, "One drunk, the other sober—that's the clever part. Not an easy match to find." When it came to Cixian's turn, he offered, "I shall propose *Flinging the Dipper* (撇斗)." Huifang said, "A fine title, *Flinging the Dipper* (撇斗)!" After a moment's thought, he replied, "I shall match it with *Searching for the Cup* (搜杯)." Cixian praised him, "And a fine answer, *Searching for the Cup* (搜杯)! The wording matches exactly. A toast, a full cup!" Everyone drank.

After a pause, Cixian pressed Huifang to produce the next title. Huifang hesitated, "I have one in mind, but I fear it may have no possible answer." Cixian encouraged him, "If there really is no match, we can drink a cup and move on. Go on, tell us. Let me think it over." Huifang explained, "*Woman Thief* (女盗) has another name: *Female Thief* (牝贼). That is an unusual two-character title. If you can produce a pairing, I will willingly drink three cups." Cixian challenged, "Is that a promise?" Everyone mentally tried and failed to come up with a match. Ziyun said, "This is a difficult title to match." Suddenly, Cixian burst out laughing and said to Huifang, "You drink your three cups first, then I shall give you the pairing." Huifang countered, "Give me the match first. Then I'll drink."

Cixian said, "You will drink. Now: *Power and Profit* (势利) also goes by the name *The Snobbish Monk* (势僧). Is that not a perfect pairing?" Huifang said, "How can 势 pair with 牝?" Ziyu thought for a moment, then clapped his hands and burst out laughing. "Marvellous, utterly marvellous! Only 势 could possibly pair with 牝. A truly perfect match." Qinyan and Baozhu had not yet understood it. Ziyun and Wang Xun, having worked it out, also began laughing and said, "A brilliant conceit, to treat those two characters as those two things: a truly inspired stroke of imagination." The four performers had not yet grasped it; Huifang was still sitting blankly thinking. Wang Xun said, "Still haven't worked it out? Don't you know that a man's member is called his 势?" At this Huifang and the others suddenly understood and began to laugh as well, all agreeing it was splendid. Huifang drank his promised three cups; everyone else drank a cup in celebration.

Ziyun then offered *Attacking the Inn* (打店), and Baozhu matched it with *Fleeing the Pass* (逃关). Baozhu proposed *Seizing the Beauty* (抢娇), which Ziyun matched with *Killing Poxi* (杀惜). Both were judged good matches and toasted with a cup. Wang Xun brought forth *Grass Bridge* (草桥), and Guibao paired it with *Hemp Ground* (麻地), then suddenly paused. "The character for 'ground' here is half a character off," he mused. "Let me change it to *The Scolding in the Pavilion* (絮阁)." Wang Xun praised the revision: "This borrowed pairing of *The Scolding in the Pavilion* (絮阁) is excellent. It deserves a half cup." Guibao next proposed *Flower Lady* (花婆). Wang Xun thought for a moment and offered *Fire Judge* (火判). The group was already praising the match and calling for a toast when Wang Xun spoke again. "Wait, let me change that," he said, and changed his match to *Grass Chancellor* (草相). The others exclaimed, "Even better! That is remarkably fresh and inventive." They all toasted in agreement. Ziyu offered *Sealing the Chamber* (封房), and Qinyan matched it with *Taking Leave of the Grand Secretary* (辞阁). This too was judged a solid pairing and rewarded with a half cup. Qinyan then proposed *Removing Armour* (卸甲). Ziyu reflected but could think of nothing entirely novel. Suddenly, he remembered a scene from *The Peach Blossom Fan* (桃花扇) called *Disrupting the Ding Sacrifice* (哄丁) and offered it as a borrowed match. Everyone was full of admiration, and each drank a full cup in toast. Cixian offered *The Eating House* (饭店), and Huifang matched it with *The Tea-House* (茶房). Huifang offered *Plucking the Eyebrow* (拔眉), and Ziyun said, "This is even harder to match." Cixian matched it with *Opening the Eye* (开眼). Huifang said, "Now that is truly clever and precise." Cixian said, "There is also *Piercing the Eye* (刺目), which I feel is rather better, except that 刺 (pierce) has an oblique tone." Ziyu said, "Both are excellent, as if heaven and earth had made them to match. You couldn't do better."

It came round to Ziyun again, and he offered *Tumbling in the Snow* (跌雪). Baozhu said, "That is a broad one, making it easy for me." He quickly added, "Though the word 'tumble' is not so easy either." After a moment's thought, he matched it with *Falling on the Ice* (堕冰). Everyone praised this in unison, "What a fine pair! *Tumbling in the Snow* (跌雪) and *Falling on the Ice* (堕冰): one idea rendered beautifully in two layers." Huifang urged Baozhu, "Give him a hard one." Baozhu nodded, but Ziyun asked, "Why put him up to making things hard for me? You just want me to drink a penalty cup." Huifang laughed, "Exactly." Ziyun challenged Baozhu, "Go on—give me your hardest." Baozhu thought for a while before offering *Supporting the Head* (扶头). Ziyun chuckled, "That is truly not easy." Then he suddenly slapped the table, "I have it! I'll match it with *Cutting the Foot* (切脚). What do you think?" Ziyu exclaimed, "Superb! Superb! This and *Plucking the Eyebrow* (拔眉) matched with *Piercing the Eye* (刺目) may both be called perfect pairings." Cixian added, "It is even better than *Plucking the Eyebrow* (拔眉) and *Piercing the Eye* (刺目). Here, 头 (head) and 脚 (foot) are both abstract, with the same underlying sense. The level and oblique tones also match well. It is a fine pairing. Since it was Meixiang who provoked it, we should toast double cups." And so everyone drank and resumed eating.

It was Wang Xun's turn again, and he offered *Flower Drum* (花鼓). Guibao thought and thought, but no match came. He grew so flustered that his face turned red.

Wang Xun pressed him. Guibao said, "I didn't expect this one to stump me. The only good match is the *Bell in the Rain* (雨铃) in *Hearing the Bells* (闻铃), but that is not an opera title. *Grass Bridge* (草桥) is not really a perfect match either, as the 'bridge' does not quite fit. I cannot think of anything else, so I shall simply drink a cup." Guibao drank half a cup, then offered *Kneeling at the Pool* (跪池). Wang Xun promptly matched it with *Leaping into the Well* (投井). Everyone approved, and half a cup was toasted. When it came to Ziyu's turn, he offered *Plucking a Willow Branch* (折柳). Ziyun laughed, "Yuxiang is making things easy for Yunong by offering such a simple title." Ziyu said, "I couldn't think of a fresh one just now. Matching a title is easier than choosing one." Qinyan matched it with *Sweeping the Pine* (扫松). Ziyu said, "Your answer makes even my opening title look good." Everyone toasted half a cup. Qinyan then proposed, "I shall take the word 'sweep' for my next title: *Sweeping Away Qin* (扫秦)." The company murmured, "Now that is a hard one." Ziyu agreed, "Very difficult indeed. 'Qin' is both a surname and the name of a state, which makes it particularly tricky." Suddenly, he thought of a match and looked quite pleased with himself. "There's a ready-made match after all! I'll give you *Checking Han* (挡汉)." Everyone exclaimed in delight, "Perfect! It is entirely natural: the characters 秦 (Qin) and 汉 (Han) pair beautifully, and the verbs 'sweep' and 'check' are matched just as well. A double toast!" By this point, they had completed three full rounds. It had been quite a long session, and the lamps had now been lit. They paused for a short rest and exchanged some light conversation.

Cixian said, "My turn again. I'll follow Yuxiang's example and offer an easy one." He offered *The Tavern* (酒楼), and Huifang matched it with *The Study* (书馆). Huifang then said, "I'll follow Yunong's linked pattern too, using 书 again in *Revising the Letter* (改书)." Cixian said, "Trying to make it hard for me? Then I'll give you a really good match." After thinking for a moment, he matched it with *Pursuing Han Xin* (追信).

Wang Xun said, "书 ('book') and 信 ('letter') form a fine pair of characters." Cixian added, "I also thought of *Releasing Yi* (放易). Yi works here much as Xin does." Everyone cried out together in praise, "That one is even better! It deserves double cups." And so they toasted. Ziyun said, "*Seeing the Ghost* (见鬼)." No one paid attention. After a pause, Baozhu pressed him to produce a title. Ziyun laughed, "You haven't answered, and now you're hurrying me?" Baozhu countered, "You haven't given a title. What am I supposed to match?" Ziyun said, "I said it just now: *Seeing the Ghost* (见鬼) is my title." Baozhu reflected, recalled that such an opera title indeed existed, and matched it with *The Departing Soul* (离魂). Ziyun nodded, "A good match." Half a cup was toasted. Baozhu then offered *Eating Chaff* (吃糠), which Ziyun matched with *Splashing Gruel* (泼粥).

It was Wang Xun's turn, and he offered *The Infernal Judge* (冥判). Cixian warned, "This is not easy. That word for 'judge' is half concrete and half abstract; Ruixiang may well be penalized." Guibao thought for a while and said, "I have a good match, though it comes from a newer play. In *Empty Valley Fragrance* (空谷香) there is a scene called *The Buddhist Healer* (佛医). I shall match it with *The Buddhist Healer* (佛医)." Cixian praised him, "Excellent! No penalty for you—you deserve a toast." Guibao then proposed, "Now I've thought of a hard one: *Startled by Ugliness* (惊丑)." Wang Xun pondered for a while and replied, "I have a good match. Compare the four characters: they have the same coloring, so you should toast double cups. I match it with *Terrifying the Simpleton* (吓痴)." Everyone laughed and agreed, "Indeed, it is dark and gloomy, exactly the same color. Double cups are in order." And so everyone toasted.

Ziyu suggested, "We can end the game here, as it is getting late. Besides, I came out early this morning. If I stay much longer, my mother may ask what kept me. Let us close with this pairing." Wang Xun agreed, "It is time. After the pairing, let us eat." Ziyun protested, "Wait, it is actually still quite early." Ziyu countered, "We plan to meet for several days. Let's save some energy for tomorrow and break up early today." In truth, Ziyu had noticed that Qinyan was beginning to look tired, and that was his real reason for wishing to end the game. Ziyun had to agree. Ziyu proposed, "Let me offer a three-character title for the close." He offered *The Flying Bear Dream* (飞熊梦). Everyone murmured, "Three characters will be harder, as fewer good matches exist." Qinyan thought for a while and matched it with *The Tiger-Subduing Scroll* (伏虎韬).

Everyone praised this warmly and drank a cup in celebration. Qinyan smiled and said, "Let us finish with just one more pairing. I shall offer a four-character title." "Four characters will be even harder," the others said. Qinyan reassured them, "The penalty for a failed match is still only one cup. If everyone had to match four-character titles, it would naturally be harder, but we may still be able to find one or two such pairings." He offered *Selling a Son and Plunging into the Abyss* (卖子投渊). Ziyu pondered for a while, then offered *Longing for One's Parents, Abandoning the Feast* (思亲罢宴). Everyone struck the table in admiration. Ziyun said, "Your words give your feelings away! Yuxiang said just now he feared his mother would ask why he was home so late, and here is *Longing for One's Parents, Abandoning the Feast* (思亲罢宴). A pairing straight from life; let us each toast three cups and then eat." Over the course of the game, each person had taken four turns, making thirty-two couplets and sixty-four opera titles in all. It had been no small effort of the mind, and dozens of cups of wine had been drunk. They were all a little tipsy and could drink no more. After drinking the three toasts, they had their meal, sat for a while, and then Ziyu and Wang Xun took their leave. Ziyun invited them back for the following day. The next day, Wenze and Chunhang joined the party, as did several more of the famous performers, and they spent another day together at the Garden of Contentment. Lu Sulan gave a small private gathering for Ziyu and Qinyan alone, a quiet, intimate day of conversation, even more delightful than the others. Over three days in a row they met and feasted, and Ziyu felt perfectly content, though by now he was quite worn out. Xu Ziyun had been planning to invite Qu Daosheng, and just at this point Shi Nanxiang arrived in the capital, providing the occasion for a grand literary gathering of wine and verse. Ziyu could not decline, and had no choice but to attend. As to what happened next, listen to the following chapter for the explanation.
